How the system works

One visible process, from market data to result.

Market data produces a signal candidate. Portfolio rules decide whether a simulated position may open. The result is then recorded for analysis.

01

Market data

Multiple timeframes, price structure, momentum, trend, and order-flow data provide the input for each cycle.

02

Signal candidate

Spot can produce a BUY candidate. Futures evaluates LONG and SHORT. NO SIGNAL remains a valid and visible result.

03

Portfolio checks

Available capital, current exposure, timing, proximity, and existing positions are checked before a simulated entry.

04

Recorded outcome

Entry, TP, SL, profit or loss, and rejection reasons are stored so the strategy can be tested and improved.

Scope and limits

Research software, not a trading service.

SmartSignalHub runs in simulation. It does not place real orders, promise profit, or provide trading instructions or financial advice.
