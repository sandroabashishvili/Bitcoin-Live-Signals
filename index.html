How the system works

From market context to an auditable outcome.

SmartSignalHub does more than display a direction. It keeps the data, decision, execution permission, and simulated result connected.

01

Market context

5m, 15m, and 4h data describe direction and market structure. One-minute data provides finer execution evidence.

02

Signal decision

Spot evaluates BUY setups. Futures evaluates LONG and SHORT setups. A clear no-signal state remains visible.

03

Permission and execution

Capital, exposure, timing, proximity, and market-zone checks are evaluated before a simulated position can open.

04

Outcome and audit

Entry, TP, SL, portfolio impact, and blocked or denied reasons remain available for review and research.

Built for traceability
